No photo needed. Some beginnings live only in stories. 🌱

Then life gave us another little chapter.

03
MORE THAN 10 YEARS LATER

Life took us in completely different directions.

Then came the long gap. More than ten years passed. Childhood became a distant memory, life moved forward, and we had no idea that the story wasn't actually over.

Sometimes a story pauses. It doesn't always end. ⏳
04
THE BUS

And then, completely unexpectedly… there you were.

After more than ten years, we met again on a bus. No grand plan. No dramatic reunion arranged by fate. Just an ordinary bus ride that quietly changed everything.

The most unexpected “hello” became the beginning of everything again. 🚌❤️
05

And somehow, the friendship found its way back.

Conversations started again. Then came more conversations, laughter, random moments, shared secrets, silly arguments and all the little things that slowly made us “us” again. And this time, I actually understood how precious it was.

06 · SOMETHING I WANTED TO SAY

To Sukanya, on your birthday

A little letter.

Hey Sukanya,

Happy Birthday to one of the most special people I know.

I hope you know how much your presence matters. There are people we meet and forget, people who stay for a chapter, and then there are people who somehow become part of the story itself.

You are one of those people for me.

Thank you for the laughter, the conversations, the nonsense, the advice, the arguments, the memories and simply for being you.

I hope this year gives you reasons to smile on ordinary days, courage for difficult ones, and many moments you will want to remember forever.

And wherever life takes us, I hope we never lose this friendship.

— Someone who is very, very lucky to call you his best friend. ❤️

07 · ONE LAST THING

Don't open this until you're ready.

There is one
little secret.

♥

You already know you're my best friend.

What you may not know is that somewhere along the way, you became a little more special to me than I ever planned.

I know you have your own priorities, your own choices, and your own life, and I respect that. I’m not expecting anything from you.

But honestly, loving you has never felt like a burden to me. Somehow, it makes me stronger. It makes me feel lighter, happier, and a little more myself whenever you’re around.

I don’t want this birthday to become complicated. I just wanted one tiny corner of the internet where, for once, I could be completely honest about what you mean to me — without expecting anything in return. ❤️

So for today, forget everything else.
Just be happy. Keep smiling. And have the most beautiful birthday. ❤️
